About the Chamber

Our Philosophy

Law practised with integrity and purpose

"Justice is not a privilege — it's a right we stand to protect."

Chaitanya Law Chamber was established with a singular purpose: to make quality legal representation accessible, understandable, and effective for every client who walks through our door.

Led by Adv. Uday P. Honguntikar, a seasoned High Court Advocate at the Kalaburagi Bench, the chamber has built a reputation for rigorous legal analysis, client-first communication, and result-oriented advocacy.

We believe the law is not just a profession — it is a public trust. Our team approaches every matter, whether a complex commercial dispute or a personal family case, with the same degree of care, preparation, and commitment.

Based in Ganesh Nagar, Kalaburagi, we serve clients across the Karnataka High Court jurisdiction and at all levels of civil and criminal courts in the region.

Our Base

Kalaburagi — a growing centre of justice

The Karnataka High Court's Kalaburagi Bench serves the Kalyana-Karnataka region — one of the most significant judicial jurisdictions in northern Karnataka. Chaitanya Law Chamber is deeply rooted in this community, having practised before this bench since its establishment.

Our location at Ganesh Nagar places us close to the courts and accessible to clients across the city and surrounding districts. We handle matters at the High Court bench, City Civil Court, District Court, and all subordinate courts in the jurisdiction.

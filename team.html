UH

Adv. Uday P. Honguntikar

Founder & Principal Advocate · High Court, Kalaburagi Bench

Adv. Uday P. Honguntikar is the founder and principal advocate of Chaitanya Law Chamber. With over 15 years of practice before the Karnataka High Court and subordinate courts in the Kalaburagi jurisdiction, he brings deep expertise in civil, criminal, family, and corporate law.

Known for his meticulous preparation, client-centered approach, and assertive advocacy, Adv. Honguntikar has successfully represented clients in hundreds of matters ranging from complex property disputes to high-stakes criminal defence. He is also a public voice on legal awareness, maintaining active outreach through social media and community engagement.
